RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT is made on July 1, 2026 between Riverside Properties LLC ("Landlord") and Jane Tenant ("Resident").

PROPERTY: 123 Main Street, Apt 4B, Springfield, IL 62701

LEASE TERM: 12 months beginning August 1, 2026 through July 31, 2027

MONTHLY RENT: $1,850.00 due on the 1st of each month.

SECURITY DEPOSIT: $1,850.00

FEES AND CHARGES:
- Administrative Fee: $150.00 (non-refundable)
- Application Fee: $45.00 per applicant
- Monthly Pet Rent: $50.00 per pet (maximum 2 pets)
- Late Fee: $75.00 after the 3rd day, increasing by $10 per day thereafter
- Returned Payment Fee: $35.00
- Lease Renewal Processing Fee: $100.00
- Parking Fee: $75.00 per vehicle per month
- Amenity Fee: $25.00 monthly (pool, gym, clubhouse access)

UTILITIES: Resident is responsible for electricity, gas, water/sewer, and internet. Landlord covers trash removal.

RENEWAL:
This lease shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice 90 days prior to expiration. Upon renewal, rent may increase by up to 8%.

EARLY TERMINATION:
Resident may terminate early by paying a fee equal to two months' rent ($3,700.00) AND forfeiting the security deposit.

MAINTENANCE:
Resident is responsible for minor repairs under $100.00. Landlord responsible for major systems. Resident must promptly report any issues in writing.

INSURANCE:
Resident must maintain renter's insurance with minimum $100,000 liability coverage.

ARBITRATION:
Any dispute arising from this lease shall be submitted to binding arbitration under the rules of the American Arbitration Association. Both parties waive the right to trial by jury.

ADDITIONAL TERMS:

- No smoking in the unit
- Quiet hours: 10 PM to 7 AM
- Landlord may enter with 24 hours notice for inspections or repairs
